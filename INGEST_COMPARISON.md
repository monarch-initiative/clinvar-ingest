## Effect on the merged Monarch KG

The KG as downloaded holds **1,582,279 nodes** and **15,807,241 edges**, with **13,191** curated gene-disease pairs.

| | main | phil-explore |
|---|---:|---:|
| Nodes contributed | 12,363 | 116,739 |
| Edges contributed | 26,082 | 321,630 |
| as % of KG edges | 0.2% | 2.0% |
| pairs corroborating a curated KG pair | 195 (70.4%) | 6,554 (73.4%) |
| pairs with no curated KG equivalent | 82 | 2,373 |

The corroboration rate moves 70.4% → 73.4% while the pair count grows 32-fold. A rate that holds steady under that much growth is the evidence that recall was not bought with precision.


## Gene-disease pairs gained and dropped

- **8,739** pairs `phil-explore` emits that `main` does not
- **89** pairs `main` emits that `phil-explore` drops
- **188** in common

Those 89 dropped pairs span 83 genes, and break down as follows:

- **65 genes this branch never attributes to any variant** — ClinVar does not assert them as causal, so they appear nowhere in its output. 23 carry an HGNC symbol:
    - antisense/divergent transcript: `CDH23-AS1`, `CFTR-AS1`, `COCH-AS1`, `EFCAB13-DT`, `GJD2-DT`, `LDLR-AS1`, `RUNX1-AS1`, `USH2A-AS1`, `USH2A-AS2`
    - miRNA: `MIR6886`
    - mitochondrial: `MT-ATP8`, `MT-ND4L`, `MT-RNR2`, `MT-TC`, `MT-TM`
    - other: `DVL2`, `MHRT`, `PKIG`, `RIF1`, `SNHG14`
    - readthrough fusion: `TBCEL-TECTA`
    - uncharacterised ORF: `C11orf65`, `C12orf43`
    - and 42 with no HGNC symbol at all — `LOC`/lncRNA/miRNA placeholders.
- **18 genes still in this branch's output**, which have simply lost one disease pairing: `AKT3`, `SLC26A1`, `LRRC56`, `COCH`, `DLG4`, `RRAS2`, `HBA1`, `HRAS`, `MT-ND2`, `MT-RNR1`, `MT-TS2`, `MT-TT`, `PIK3R2`, `CCDC40`, `WRAP53`, `SCN1B`, `CDH23`, `CDKL5`

> **Worth a reviewer's eye:** 5 mitochondrial gene(s) — `MT-ATP8`, `MT-ND4L`, `MT-RNR2`, `MT-TC`, `MT-TM` — fall in the first group, so this branch emits nothing for them. Other MT genes do survive (`MT-ATP6`, `MT-CO1`, `MT-CO2`, `MT-CO3`, `MT-CYB`, `MT-ND1`, `MT-ND2`, `MT-ND3`, …), so this is not a blanket exclusion of the mitochondrial genome, but it is the part of the drop that is least obviously an artifact and should be confirmed before merge.
